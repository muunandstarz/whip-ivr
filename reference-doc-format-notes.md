# Reference Document Format Notes

## KlutchCOI.pdf

The certificate is a **portrait** single-page layout. The header places the Klutch logo at the top left, the title **CERTIFICATE OF COVERAGE** centered, and the date plus certificate number at the top right. A subtitle line states that the document is issued by Klutch Insurance Company pursuant to the relevant state transportation article.

Below the header is a full-width disclaimer box, followed by a three-column grid for producer/carrier, contact, and insurer information. The named insured area shows **METROCARS LEASING CORP**, the named operator/lessee, and the vehicle with VIN. The right side shows coverage period and state of loss.

The coverages section includes a certificate number row, a disclaimer paragraph, and a structured table with checkbox columns and rows for **BI, PD, UM, UIM, PIP, COL, and COMP**. The footer area includes a certificate holder box on the left and cancellation text on the right.

## Barkley_Dec_Page.pdf

The declarations packet is **portrait** and spans **two pages**. It uses a left main content column and a right sidebar column. Page 1 begins with **Policy Declarations**, then policy number and effective date, with the Klutch logo at top right.

The main body contains a vehicle/member summary and a coverage table with columns for limits, deductible, and premium. The sidebar contains summary information, claim reporting instructions, cancellation notice, fraud warning, and renewal notice. The page ends with a footer line containing Klutch company contact details.

Page 2 contains **Policy conditions & important notices**, an **Authorized representative** block with signature/date, and a right sidebar with vehicle summary, territory/garaging, information-correction notice, and premium notice.

## Release Templates

The BI general release, PD general release, and limited BI release should be generated from the attached reference formats directly, with field substitution only. They should not depend on AI validation or AI-generated release text.

## Subro Demand Letter

The attached subro demand format should be used as the formatting reference. Required changes from the sample are: remove the dark black header line, left-align the date, and remove the phrase **for settlement purposes**.
