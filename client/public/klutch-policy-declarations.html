Policy conditions & important notices

The following conditions govern coverage under this policy. All terms are subject to the master policy and applicable state law.

1. Insuring Agreement. This policy is issued by Klutch Insurance Company to Metrocars Leasing Corp (named insured and vehicle owner/lessor). The named operator is an insured while operating the covered vehicle pursuant to an active Membership Agreement with Metrocars Leasing Corp.
2. Premium. Premium for all coverages is included in the member's weekly Membership Fee paid to Metrocars Leasing Corp. No separate premium invoice is issued.
3. Physical Damage Deductibles. Collision and comprehensive deductibles correspond to the Member Damage Fee defined in the Membership Agreement between the named operator and Metrocars Leasing Corp.
4. Personal Injury Protection (PIP). PIP is provided only in states where required by law. In Maryland, the named operator may execute a PIP waiver or elect Enhanced PIP in accordance with the Maryland Transportation Article. Where PIP is not mandated, this coverage does not apply.
5. Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM). UM and UIM coverages are provided only where required by applicable state law. In states permitting rejection (e.g., Georgia), these coverages do not apply unless elected. Limits equal the statutory minimum for the state of vehicle operation at the time of loss.
6. Authorized Operators Only. Coverage applies exclusively to the named operator listed on this declarations page. Unauthorized or unlisted operators are excluded from all coverages.
7. Other Insurance. If other valid and collectible coverage applies to a covered loss, this policy coordinates with such coverage in accordance with applicable law and the Membership Agreement.
8. Continuity of Coverage. Coverage is contingent upon an active Membership Agreement. Termination or cancellation of the Membership Agreement immediately terminates all coverage under this policy.
